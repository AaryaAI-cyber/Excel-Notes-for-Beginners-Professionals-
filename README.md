## Why This Repository Stands Out

- **Business-first curriculum.** Every module opens with a job-relevant use case and closes with a decision-ready example, not a feature list.
- **From formulas to the modern data stack.** Dynamic arrays, LET and LAMBDA, Power Query, Power Pivot and DAX, Python in Excel, and Office Scripts.
- **Financial modeling depth.** Loan schedules, NPV and IRR, DCF, budgeting, variance analysis, depreciation, and scenario planning.
- **AI with guardrails.** Copilot and Claude in Excel workflows, prompt patterns, privacy rules, and a seven-point verification checklist.
- **Portfolio-grade capstones.** Five end-to-end projects with datasets, rubrics, and publishing guidance for sales, HR, inventory, marketing, and QA.
- **Interview-ready.** 206 questions with answers, ten timed practical tasks, and a four-week preparation plan.
- **Automated quality gates.** Every daily publish checks links, images, workbook integrity, local paths, and lesson structure before it reaches `main`.

## How to Use This Repo

### For learners

1. Start at **Module 01** and work in order.
2. Read the module `README.md`, then open `practice_workbook.xlsx` and follow the `Demo` sheet.
3. Do the exercises in `exercises_and_solutions.md` before opening the solutions.
4. Finish with the five capstone projects in **Module 33** and the interview drills in **Module 34**.

### For analysts and finance teams

1. Use the **Analyst** or **Finance** learning path above.
2. Focus on lookups, dynamic arrays, Power Query, Power Pivot, modeling, and auditing.
3. Keep [CHEATSHEET.md](CHEATSHEET.md) open while you work.
4. Use the capstone projects as templates for your own portfolio.

### For interviewers and hiring managers

1. Use **Module 33** to evaluate end-to-end business thinking.
2. Use **Module 34** and [INTERVIEW_QUESTIONS.md](INTERVIEW_QUESTIONS.md) for structured technical screens.
3. Every module includes five interview questions with answers and a practical exercise set.

## What Is Inside Every Module

```text
NN_topic_name/
├── README.md                     the lesson notes
├── practice_workbook.xlsx        sample data and working formulas
├── exercises_and_solutions.md    8 to 10 exercises with full solutions
└── images/                       diagrams used in the notes
```

Every lesson follows the same seven-part structure:

1. **Objective and Business Use Case** — why the skill matters in a real job.
2. **Core Concepts, Syntax and Jargon Buster** — plain-English definitions and the exact formulas.
3. **Step-by-Step Practical Walkthrough** — a guided build in Excel.
4. **Real-World Scenarios and Worked Examples** — formula, result, and business interpretation.
5. **Common Pitfalls and Performance Tips** — what breaks and how to avoid it.
6. **Interview Questions with Solutions** — five questions with model answers.
7. **Practice Exercises with Solutions** — eight to ten tasks with hidden solutions.

## Workbook Conventions

- **Green header row:** column names.
- **Blue text:** inputs you can change.
- **Yellow cells:** your answer area.
- **`Demo` sheet:** column C shows the formula as text and column D shows the live result.
- **All data is fictional.** Any resemblance to real people or companies is a coincidence.

## Quality and Accuracy

- Every formula result in the notes was recalculated and cross-checked against an independent calculation.
- Every function is tagged with the minimum Excel version that supports it.
- Every link, image, workbook, and local path is checked by the daily publishing pipeline before it reaches `main`.
- Functions that need Microsoft 365 or a newer Excel version are clearly marked.
- The reference guides were verified against current documentation on **7 November 2026**. Product names, plans, and limits change, so confirm them on the vendor's pages.

## Daily Automation

The repository is updated by a Windows Task Scheduler job at **21:30 local time**:

1. Fetch and rebase from `origin/main` with autostash.
2. Pick the next scheduled module in sequence.
3. Validate its links, images, workbook, local paths, and lesson structure.
4. Copy the module into the repository.
5. Update the roadmap status and progress block in this README.
6. Commit with a conventional message such as `docs: add Module 01 - Excel Basics and Interface`.
7. Push to `main`, with automatic rollback if the push fails.
8. Skip cleanly if a module was already published that day.

The publisher is documented in [scripts/README.md](scripts/README.md). A timestamped log is written to the local workspace as `excel-notes-daily.log`.
